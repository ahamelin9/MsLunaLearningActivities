# Principles

Why Ms. Luna looks and behaves the way it does. Everything else in these docs
follows from these.

## Rules

1. **ESL reading first.** This is an ESL teacher's app, and reading is the
   core. No school subjects, ever. Later apps are ESL companions (Writing is
   next, WRT).
2. **Made by hand, not generated.** Flat paper shapes, drawn icons, a little
   texture. No gradient washes, no glassy pills, no emoji standing in for icons.
3. **Boho, in sand and cocoa.** Light tans and browns, soft arches, pampas
   grass. No rainbow.
4. **Heard, never shown.** Targets and answers are spoken, never written before
   the round is solved. Hints are spoken. Where space is tight, Luna *says* it
   instead of showing a bubble.
5. **Simple for a five-year-old.** Big targets, one clear way in to everything,
   one app on the home screen. Grown-up things sit behind the gate.
6. **Calm until it matters.** Motion is for feedback, wins and drawing the eye.
   Words a child is reading never move.
7. **Progress you can see, simply.** Lessons go in order, and each one is
   plainly done, up next or locked. Time is shown before and after.
8. **Collecting is joyful.** Squishies with rare colours, a shelf with things
   still to find, and a secret last one.
9. **Light and dark from day one.** Everything is built on roles, so every
   screen works in both.
10. **The moon is the one colour that breaks the palette.** Moon gold, so a moon
    reads as a moon. Nothing else gets to.

## Two places, one family

| | Home screen | Reading app |
|---|---|---|
| **Feel** | An iPad: status bar, the app icon, home indicator | Luna's boho classroom: warm paper, arches, pampas |
| **Ground** | Sand dunes and sky; the sun by day, the crescent moon and stars at night | Oat paper with a faint grain |
| **Motion** | Slow and ambient: drifting sky, swaying grass | Feedback and celebration; calm while a child reads |

Both use the same roles, type, icons and kit. Opening Reading should feel like
opening an app on the same iPad, not visiting another website.

## Who decides

- **The teacher** picks the look. It's her app. Alex shows her the style sample
  and passes her feedback on.
- **Alex** sets priorities, approves the standards and signs off anything that
  needs judgement: how something looks or sounds, and a new role or primitive.
- **Claude** proposes, builds and checks, and records every decision in
  [decisions.md](decisions.md).

Her tastes so far: light tans and browns, never rainbow. Simple and literal wins;
she pushed back on anything that needs decoding (seeds growing into flowers). She
loved the round-framed owl and prefers light mode, but dark has to be shown too.

## Where the look came from

The full redesign started on 2026-10-08, when the app read as a generic
AI-made site. Round 1 offered four directions (Boho Classroom, Moonlit, Groovy
Garden, Clear) and eight characters for Luna. The teacher chose Boho, recoloured
into light tans and browns. Round 2 settled the rest: the owl, the milestone
moon, one progress bar, squishies and the home screen. She approved it on
2026-10-10. The [style sample](https://claude.ai/artifact/Gr1ngbrVdGS6xE4pALyWsm)
(page "Round 2 · Teacher's picks") is the frozen reference. The dated record is
in [decisions.md](decisions.md).
