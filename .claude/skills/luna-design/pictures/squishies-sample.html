  3. Today every kind's super-rare is honey. My pick: each kind has one super-rare look, so a kind stays at 3 colours and the shelf's dots don't change. A child's saved honey squishy would show as that kind's look.
  4. Which kinds also get a gentle moving version. My pick: three, one for each look: Little Moon, Cloud and Heart.
  5. Which kinds show as a "?" until they're found, while the rest show as a grey shape. My pick: Toast, Acorn, Star and Penguin, where the board has its "?" slots, plus Butter Block and Little Moon, which already do.
